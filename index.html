About

Engineering that holds up in production.

I'm a full-stack software engineer based in India. Over roughly eight years I've worked across the whole application — Angular and React frontends, Node.js, .NET and Go backends, SQL and NoSQL data stores, and the CI/CD that ships it all.

I care about the parts that make software trustworthy: authentication and authorisation done properly, clear system boundaries, sensible trade-offs, and interfaces that people actually enjoy using. I like to explain the why behind a design as much as build it.

Right now I'm deepening my work in system design, cloud (GCP) and applied GenAI / agentic systems, and I'm looking for senior or lead roles at product companies where I can own problems end to end and mentor others.
